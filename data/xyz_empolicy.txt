LEAVE
• Annual leave: 18 working days/year.
• Sick leave: 10 days/year; notify your manager promptly.
• Casual leave: 6 days/year.
• Public holidays follow the location calendar.
• Ask HR about parental leave eligibility and duration.
• Unpaid leave needs manager and HR approval.
• Apply 5 working days ahead through the HR portal when possible. Report emergencies promptly.
• Up to 5 unused annual-leave days may carry forward with approval. Sick and casual leave do not.
• Leave is not approved until confirmed.

WORKING HOURS
Monday–Friday, 9:30 a.m.–6:00 p.m., including a 30-minute lunch. Approved team schedules may differ. Record attendance and get overtime approval.

REMOTE WORK
Up to 2 remote days weekly with manager approval. Use approved devices and protect company data.

CONDUCT
Treat colleagues respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation are prohibited. Report concerns to HR or a manager.

SECURITY
Use strong passwords and MFA. Never share credentials. Store files in approved systems and report phishing, lost devices, or data exposure to IT.

AI AND PRIVACY
Use approved AI tools only. Never enter confidential, personal, or customer data into unapproved services. Check AI output. Access confidential data only for authorized work.

EXPENSES
Get approval before business purchases. Submit receipts within 30 days. Keep company equipment secure and return it when requested or when employment ends.

PERFORMANCE AND HELP
Managers should set clear goals and provide feedback. Contact HR about benefits, leave, or workplace concerns. If a policy is unclear, consult current approved documents or ask HR. Never assume an undocumented exception is approved.
